# Soft-Magic AI Product Context

register: product

## Product Purpose

Soft-Magic AI is a SaaS studio for small and medium businesses that need to turn product details, brand assets, and rough scripts into short-form ad video concepts. The app supports a practical workflow: create an ad project, generate or review a storyboard, render campaign assets, manage credits and billing, and inspect admin safety surfaces.

## Users

Primary users are SMB operators, founders, marketers, and agency staff who need usable campaign output quickly without managing a full production pipeline. They are likely comparing project status, editing scripts, approving scenes, and checking credit usage between other business tasks.

## Brand And Tone

Soft-Magic AI should feel calm, capable, and commercially useful. The product tone is direct and task-focused, with enough warmth to make ad creation approachable. UI copy should avoid vague AI promises and keep the user oriented around concrete campaign progress.

## Strategic Principles

- Prioritize task clarity over decorative novelty.
- Keep the authenticated app dense enough for repeated use.
- Make every state legible: draft, generating, review, approved, failed, and completed.
- Treat demo mode as a first-class local experience, not a degraded fallback.
- Keep controls familiar so users can focus on campaign content.

## Anti-References

- Generic AI SaaS landing patterns with inflated metrics and decorative gradients.
- Over-rounded glass panels that reduce contrast or make forms feel soft.
- Marketing copy inside workflow screens that restates what the page already does.
- Hidden or ambiguous destructive and admin actions.
