# Soft-Magic AI Design Context

## Register

Product UI. Design serves campaign creation, review, billing, settings, and admin workflows.

## Theme

Users work in normal office light on laptops, often while switching between ad accounts, ecommerce dashboards, and product files. Use a light interface with warm tinted neutrals, strong text contrast, and restrained accent color.

## Color

Use OKLCH tokens for a warm neutral system. Keep the palette restrained: neutral surfaces carry most of the app, with a single amber-orange accent reserved for primary actions, focus, and selected states. Semantic colors should be muted enough to work on pale backgrounds.

## Typography

Use a product-appropriate sans stack for most UI. Keep labels, controls, and tables compact. Mono is acceptable for metadata, short IDs, and status eyebrows, but should not dominate page hierarchy.

## Shape And Surfaces

Use modest radii for task UI, preferably 8 to 16px. Avoid nested cards and broad glassmorphism. Prefer solid, lightly tinted panels with clear borders and predictable spacing.

## Components

Buttons, inputs, selects, pills, nav items, and cards should share one visual vocabulary. Interactive states need visible hover, focus, disabled, and loading treatment. Forms should have readable labels and help text, with accessible focus rings and error states.

## Layout

The authenticated app should use a stable sidebar plus content column on desktop, collapsing cleanly on small screens. Page headers should be compact and leave room for the user task. Data and workflow screens should favor scanability over large promotional composition.
